[Presentation on the 11th March 2026, A.5.1]

We present a reliability framework for cuffless blood pressure estimation that assigns a confidence score to each prediction. The approach uses physiologically meaningful signal features to estimate how trustworthy a BP estimate is. It is designed to work alongside existing models and support reliability-aware filtering and quality control in continuous BP monitoring systems.
